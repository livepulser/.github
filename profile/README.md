## What is LivePulser?

**LivePulser is a real-time uptime and reliability monitoring platform that watches your websites, servers, APIs, SSL certificates, domains, ports, and cron jobs around the clock — and alerts you the instant any of them go down, slow down, or behave abnormally.** It's built for developers, DevOps teams, agencies, and businesses that need to know about downtime *before their users do*.

Instead of finding out about an outage from an angry customer, LivePulser checks your services continuously from multiple regions and notifies you in seconds through Slack, Discord, or email — so you can fix issues before they cost you traffic, revenue, or trust.

## What we monitor

LivePulser is a single platform for everything that needs to stay up:

| Service | What it does |
|---|---|
| 🌐 **Website &amp; uptime monitoring** | Continuously checks your sites are online and responding correctly |
| 🖥️ **Server &amp; API monitoring** | Verifies servers and API endpoints are reachable and returning healthy responses |
| 🔒 **SSL certificate monitoring** | Warns you *before* certificates expire, so HTTPS never silently breaks |
| 🌍 **Domain expiry monitoring** | Tracks domain registration dates so you never lose a domain by surprise |
| ⏰ **Cron job monitoring** | Detects missed or failed scheduled jobs ("catch every skip") |
| 🔌 **Port monitoring** | Checks specific TCP/UDP ports and services are open and responsive |
| 📊 **Resource monitoring** | Tracks CPU, RAM, and disk usage to catch problems before they cause outages |
| 🛰️ **Multi-region checks** | Confirms availability from several locations to rule out false alarms |
| 🔔 **Instant alerts** | Real-time notifications via Slack, Discord &amp; email |
| 📃 **Public status pages** | Shareable status pages that keep your users and team informed |

## Why LivePulser?

- **All-in-one** — uptime, SSL, domains, cron jobs, ports, and resources in one dashboard instead of five separate tools.
- **Fast, real-time alerts** — get notified in seconds, not minutes, through the channels you already use.
- **Fewer false alarms** — multi-region checks confirm an outage is real before paging you.
- **Built for everyone** — simple enough for a solo founder's side project, deep enough for a DevOps team's production stack.
- **Proactive, not reactive** — SSL and domain expiry warnings catch the silent failures that take sites down without a single error in your logs.
